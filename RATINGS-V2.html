04 The residue what's still wrong
Self-assessed evidence
The skill map's ratings are the agent rating its own teaching. The evidence format helps; the bias remains.
Pedagogy still unproven
Two days of data. The mechanism is excellent; whether lessons convert to durable skill is still a hypothesis.
Cross-folder dependency
explainify's progression spec lives in promptify's folder. Still one delete away from breakage.
Junior adoption wall
Lite and quick modes help, but a newcomer still faces nine markdown files and "topology." The wall is shorter, not gone.
Render step remains manual
game-render.js is invoked by hand per activation. Fine at this scale; the temptation to skip grows at 30+ entries.
05 The verdict
“
The retouches are not cosmetic — they are the v1 critique, implemented. The set went from 8.5 → 8.8, and the shape of the criticism changed: v1 was about ceremony and gimmickry; v2 is about measurement and evidence — which is what a mature system should worry about. The teaching cluster's pivot from game to competency map is the bravest and best change in the set.
Recommendation · run the skill map two weeks, then re-rate on evidence, not design
