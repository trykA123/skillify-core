# Ten questions before the answers — the ELI10 walkthrough

## What happened
During the architecture review, the questions arrived before the answers — specific, grounded, anchored to known things, and sized to the moment. The concurrency model, the script choice, the database consolidation, the client strategy, and the TV packaging were each interrogated with one sharp question before any ratification.

## What worked
- Questions named their objects: the encode count, the stack, the wrapper, the speed difference — never vague
- The trace-before-ask pattern held: files and pages were read or pointed at before the question
- New concepts were anchored to known ones, so the answers landed on existing ground
- The tired-moment ask was honest about its size — a ten-year-old-level walkthrough, delivered plainly

- Nothing in this record — the questioning pattern held all night; the gaps it exposed are recorded in the sibling records
