# The architecture night — intent, constraints, and verification asks

## What happened
One long session moved the house's transcode engine from design to ratified architecture, parked a cloud-model experiment honestly, restored the fleet's original model matrix, silenced a recurring notification, and captured nineteen owner-ratified ideas for the brain console. The prompting was intent-led throughout: single-sentence asks with the what first, constraints stated before work, anti-examples with reasons attached, and verification demanded at the decision points.

## What worked
- The ask discipline held even while tired — the shortest asks carried the most complete intent
- Constraints travelled with the asks: schedules untouched, credentials out of chat, no agent pushes — and the agent honored them
- Priority and scope were stated, so the roadmap's order was never guessed
- The verification asks produced honest answers — including one that exposed a false positive

## What didn't
- One verification ask was answered with a mirage — proof celebrated before origin was checked, caught by the logs
- A delivered batch was judged harshly because no pre-delivery check existed — the process gap, not the taste, was the failure
