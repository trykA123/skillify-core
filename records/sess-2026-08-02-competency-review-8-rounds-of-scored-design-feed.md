# Competency review — 8 rounds of scored design feedback, full map rated

## What happened
Eight rounds of scored design feedback reviewed the full skill map, each round naming what worked and what didn't with a score and a locator. The review itself became the evidence — every competency rated from a real moment.

## What worked
- Rejected two concepts with the reason attached — too similar, hard to navigate
- the other 2 concepts are similar — the pattern named, not just felt
- not the same header + legend — wrong direction killed with the reason attached
- Asked which two skills would work best together — a specific pair, a specific ask
- Centered the element under the arrows — DOM-level specificity
- Proposed using the element colors as a concrete alternative
- Named two candidate tools and probed each capability — specific, not generic
- Affirmed the accepted concept, then named the one push left to perfect it — built on known ground
- Confirmed shared understanding — referenced the context instead of re-explaining
- Workflow recap was accurate from memory — built on known ground truth

## What didn't
- Left the out-of-rule items untouched — the anti-example held, though not yet extended to every guard
