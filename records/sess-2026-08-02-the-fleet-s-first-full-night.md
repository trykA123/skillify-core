# The fleet's first full night

## What happened
Eight orchestrator runs completed in a single night while the owner slept: the dashboard rebuild closed out, the polish round shipped (with a preemptive taste pass), the seeding watcher harvested its first real deletions (41, with the ledger record), the agents tab and case studies landed, the feature round and charts reinvention shipped (two researcher debuts), the skill map built and deployed behind the edge, and the fleet ledger rendered its first analytics. The parent gate reviewed every run in the owner's place and fixed what the owner would have found — the contrast failures, the off-scale rems, the misleading numbers.

## What worked
- The review loop measured instead of eyeballed: computed contrast, scale audits, honest-number checks — the annoying pass caught a fallback literal and off-scale labels before the owner saw them
- The race guard held under real pressure: two orchestrators touched the same file, one writer discipline won
- The privacy gate caught its own breach — verbatim speech in stored records was found, the gate was rebuilt stronger, and the re-audit came back clean
- The owner's two-word authorization triggered the whole teaching loop, and recordify's first real record is this one

## What didn't
- The skill map's first launch needed a refresh before anything rendered — a worker-layout race, parked for the revision plan
- The beta's map was hub-first; the owner's vision was sessions-first — the oneshot missed it, which is exactly what oneshots do without owner feedback
- The records once held verbatim speech — fixed forward, and the history-scrub decision stays parked
