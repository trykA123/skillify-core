# Challenge claims with evidence; delegate the how, lock the what

**Principle:** Challenge claims with evidence; delegate the how, lock the what.

**Why:** When a claim contradicts observed reality, the move is to bring the numbers and demand reconciliation — the stated claim, the observed number, the demand to agree. A cost model off by roughly ten times was caught that way, and cache-read pricing surfaced in the same pass. Delegation works the same way: constraint, constraint, then an explicit grant of choice — four grants landed once with zero round-trips.

**When to apply:** pricing and estimate claims, and delegating implementation where the what must survive the how.

**When not to:** when the claim is already verified and the demand to agree would be theatre.
