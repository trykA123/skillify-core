# Research first, design second, build third

**Principle:** Research first, design second, build third.

**Why:** The charts reinvention ran the researcher's first flight: about thirty sources, fifteen dropped as fluff, and a verdict to keep the existing chart library at its current major version — the rejected alternatives were measured on bundle, feature, and render grounds. A measurable pattern catalog replaced taste talk, and the median chart was hand-verified against the live database.

**When to apply:** design decisions with ecosystem options, where the cost of the wrong library or architecture is measured in rewrites.

**When not to:** known-solution trivial work where research would only delay a settled choice.
